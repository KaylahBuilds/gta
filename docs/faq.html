FAQ

Answers, mostly short

INSTALLING
It says loaded but F5 does nothing.
Another mod owns F5. Change keys.operations in config.json to anything free and press Insert to reload.
Nothing loads at all on Enhanced.
Two usual causes: you're on standard ScriptHookVDotNet instead of the Enhanced build, or BattlEye is still on. Launch without it. Install guide.
Every script in my game broke after installing.
You almost certainly have a second copy of ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll in the scripts folder. It belongs in the game root only — delete the one in scripts.
PLAYING
Where do I find people to recruit?
Prospects are blipped while you're standing in gang turf. Walk a block if the search comes back empty — the pool refreshes on a timer, not on demand.
My whole block got shut down. What happened?
Heat hit the ceiling and vice turned the corner over. Everyone comes off the street and it stays closed for days. Bribe it down before it gets there, or leave the corner unstaffed and let it cool.
Is the weekly deposit just free money?
It only builds while someone is off the street, so it costs you their hourly take. It's safe, not free.
Can I lose?
Yes. Unpaid wages, bail and fines become debt, debt compounds, and eventually the people you owe come collecting.
THE BUILD
Why is it behind Patreon?
It funds the time. Any paid tier gets the file — Patreon does the membership check itself, so there's no key and nothing to activate.
Online?
No, and don't try. Script mods in GTA Online get accounts banned.
Will saves survive updates?
Through 0.x, assume they might not. Balance is still moving and some changes reset the roster. It'll settle before 1.0.
How explicit is it?
It isn't. Adult themes handled the way the base game handles them — abstracted, fade to black. Every character is an adult. What's modelled is money, hours, heat and territory.
ONE WORLD + ACCESS
What is One World?
It is the connected ecosystem joining TrapStar, Swipe Life, On The Blade, and the upcoming Backdoored. Each mod can stand alone; installed together, supported systems exchange city state such as pressure, reputation, money movement, and property risk.
Is Backdoored available now?
Not yet. Backdoored is in development and marked coming soon everywhere it appears. Current paid access covers the released collection; its preview page explains the planned home-invasion and security systems.
What does the One World Discord role unlock?
It unlocks the private One World members room and every individual mod room. Individual roles remain available for players who only need announcements and support for one title.
Can I install only one mod?
Yes. Start with one, confirm it loads, then add the others one at a time. Do not duplicate shared dependencies when adding another mod.
Still stuck?
Post in the correct Discord support room with your GTA edition, mod name and version, SHVDN version, what you expected, what happened, and the last relevant lines of ScriptHookVDotNet.log. Remove personal paths or account details before uploading logs.
