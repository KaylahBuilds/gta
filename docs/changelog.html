CHANGELOG

What moved, and when

Balance changes land in config.json — you can pull them without waiting for a build.

v0.2.0
CURRENT
2 AUGUST 2026
NEW
Street reputation. Five ranks from Nobody to Untouchable, moved by every incident you resolve or drop. It gates tier-3 prospects, discounts protection and lifts the loyalty new signings arrive with.
NEW
Protection deals. Pay a crew and they leave your corners alone for a few days. Poaching from a crew you are paying voids the deal — and they keep the money.
NEW
Demand events. Concerts, conventions, paydays, crackdowns and dead nights move one zone between x0.35 and x1.9 for a few hours.
NEW
Territory on the map. Every corner marked and coloured by who holds it, with staffing and raid countdowns in the label.
NEW
Worker traits. Hustler, Camera-ready, Connected, Fragile, Loyal, Magnetic — nought to two each, every one a trade.
NEW
Shifts. Always, days, nights or off, separate from where someone is posted. Off-shift hours build followers and stamina.
NEW
Zone saturation. Each worker on a corner earns less than the last, so spreading out now beats stacking the best zone.
NEW
Debt and collapse. Wages, bail and fines you cannot cover compound at 8% a day. At $150,000 the people you owe come collecting.
NEW
Raids. Past 95% heat a staffed corner gets turned over — everyone off the street, the block shut for days, and a fine.
NEW
Bribes, milestones, and a vehicle per region. Ten goals from your first signing to breaking every crew.
NEW
Muscle keeps rivals off your corners. An enforcer turns away 27% to 45% of moves on turf they cover.
CHANGE
Recruiting is far less sparse. The eligible pool went from 7 models to 86 and the search radius from 20m to 35m, or 70m on gang turf and around Vinewood.
CHANGE
Objectives name the place and the person. "Defend Sandy Shores" rather than "defend your corner".
CHANGE
Default keys are F5 and F1, clear of the block the common modding stack already claims.
CHANGE
The recruit menu says why nobody qualifies instead of a bare "nobody".
FIX
A takeover could not be won even after clearing the street. Winning required every enforcer to register as dead, so one who fled or fell through the map blocked it forever.
FIX
Config, saves and logs were written to the wrong folder, so a correctly placed config was never read and the mod silently ran on defaults.
FIX
New games started with no rivals and no territory — a first run had no crews, no poaching and no turf war.
FIX
Crew posted to the same corner stood inside each other.
FIX
Incident timers ran during deaths and cutscenes, so you could regain control to an already-failed objective.
FIX
Rival enforcers spawned at unverified coordinates, and the Vespucci and Strawberry corners were in the wrong place.
BUILD
Saves are versioned and migrate automatically. A 0.1.0 save loads into 0.2.0 with roster, territory and money intact.
Known: corner placement in Mirror Park, Del Perro and Vinewood is still unverified, and balance is tuned by simulation rather than by enough real play. Both are being worked through.
