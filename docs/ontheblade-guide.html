First 15 minutes

Start with one person and one useful corner

Open F5 and read the board

Check roster capacity, cash, debt, open territory, heat, and the currently highlighted objective.

Find a recruit in a hotspot

Gang turf favors volume; Vinewood favors higher tiers. Approach an eligible prospect and use the recruitment flow.

Choose a sustainable shift

Nights are the safest default. Stamina recovers off duty, and exhausted workers lose loyalty and earning power.

Post to a neutral corner

Working neutral ground claims it. Stay close enough to learn the income, traffic, heat, and incident rhythm.

Bank a reserve

Do not immediately buy a lease or stack wages. Keep enough cash for problems, fines, bail, and one bad night.

Roster

The stat bars are decisions

STAMINA

Hours have a cost

Rest creates the next profitable shift. Running someone empty damages loyalty and output.

LOYALTY

Retention changes risk

Pay, presence, incidents, poaching, and promises shape whether someone stays.

TRAITS

Fit the person to the work

Hustlers, camera-ready workers, fragile workers, and experienced recruits create different best assignments.

MUSCLE

Protection is payroll

Enforcers, gear, vehicles, and standing orders reduce losses but keep billing even on quiet days.

Territory + properties

Expansion creates fixed costs

Owned corners pay better but invite rival pressure while staffed. Houses trade street heat for rent, occupancy limits, and raid exposure. Content houses turn audience into safer income but still wear down and require management.

Expansion test: if losing one worker or one corner would make wages and rent unaffordable, the business is not ready for another property.
