The fourth pillar

Build the score.
Or become the target.

Backdoored is being designed as a two-sided property system: case and invade homes for high-risk rewards, while the city can also threaten the locations that power your empire.

01 / CASE

Learn the property.

Observe routines, discover entry points, read security, and decide whether the potential take is worth the exposure.

02 / BREACH

Choose how you enter.

Quiet tools, forced entries, alarms, occupants, and response windows make every property a different job.

03 / TAKE

Carry what matters.

Cash, inventory, valuables, and evidence create hard choices while the clock—and the neighborhood—keeps moving.

04 / CONSEQUENCE

The city remembers.

Damage, stolen stock, repairs, security upgrades, heat, and retaliation flow back into the shared One City save.
