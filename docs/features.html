08 · INDOORS

A house earns two to three times the street rate and draws almost no heat. It also has rooms, and rooms run out — there is no crowding curve to lean on, just a wall. And rent falls due whether anybody worked or not.

A house pays no night bonus. The street pays 1.6× after dark; a house pays flat around the clock — so the same worker is worth more indoors by day and more on a corner by night.
And it is a much fatter target. Every room let, and it goes over in about four game days: everyone out, five days shut, a $25,000 fine, and the rent still running. Hold one room back and it stays quiet almost indefinitely.
09 · CLIENTS

Somebody asked for her by name

None of this is an errand. Regulars pay quietly in the background, and a booking is a decision taken from the phone with a fade to black at the end of it.

Regulars
Clients who come back for her specifically, paying on top of every hour she works. The only income that rewards leaving somebody in one place instead of chasing demand — and it falls away if you bench her.
Bookings
She is gone four to nine hours earning nothing else, for roughly 2.3× what those hours were worth on the street. Tier, traits and loyalty decide who gets asked for.
RISK SCALES WITH THE MONEY
Somebody connected
A vice lieutenant, somebody's lawyer, a producer. Take the money — or take the favour instead: heat off every corner you hold, a week of peace with whoever hates you most, or an audience.
Somebody who checks
$11,000
A name run before anyone is booked. You see how bad a client is likely to be before you say yes, instead of after.
10 · CREW

People you keep, not people you cycle

Tier used to be rolled once at recruit and never moved again, despite being the largest term in the payout formula. Now it can be worked for, bought, taught — and it eventually runs out.

Promotion
Thirty street hours and 65 loyalty earns tier 2; ninety earns tier 3. She will not step up while she has checked out, so loyalty gates the climb.
Invest in one person
A wardrobe buys the promotion outright. A doctor, studio time, papers. None of it survives her leaving — which is the point.
Run a corner
A tier-3 worker can manage instead of work. She earns nothing; everyone else there earns more and draws less heat, scaled by her own loyalty.

Teaching
A junior posted with somebody better gains double experience. The senior gains nothing, so who you put together is a decision rather than a sum.
Wanting out
Past four hundred hours she may ask to leave, and anyone can want out for reasons that have nothing to do with you. Let her go well, pay her to stay, or tell her no — and each costs something different.
11 · THE LAW

Heat with a face on it

Heat used to have two levers, both of them menu buttons. Now there is somebody to pay, somebody selling you out, and a courthouse that takes your crew away for days at a time.

A man at the station
$2,500/day
A raid becomes a phone call — six hours' notice naming the corner. He warns; he does not cancel. Let it lapse and he puts everything he knows on somebody's desk.
Somebody talking
A worker below 55 loyalty starts helping the police, and her corner runs hot. You are told the moment it starts — but not who. Pay to have everyone checked, or guess and lose an innocent along with everyone else's trust.
Custody
Without the bail fund a bust means two to six days inside, depending on how hot the corner was. She earns nothing and cannot be posted. A lawyer buys the rest of it, and she knows who paid.
12 · THE CREWS

They do things back now

The relationship ran one way in every respect — you took their people and their corners, and the worst they managed was turning up occasionally.

They take one of yours
At 70 loyalty she refuses outright and gains ten for telling you about it. Below that it scales to certain at zero. Loyalty is a defence now, not just a multiplier.
War
Push a crew far enough and they stop testing you. Constant contests, more bodies each time, and no peace at the ordinary price. It ends when one of you gives it up.
CONTESTS ×2.2
Alliances
With enough of a name a crew can be brought all the way over rather than merely bought off — and they send people to fight beside you when you go and take somebody else's corner.

14 · DEBT

The way to lose

Borrowing on purpose
Cash in hand now, $1.45 on the book for every dollar of it, compounding at 8% a day like everything else you owe. Nobody will lend you enough to end the run in one transaction.
Collectors
Past $82,500 owed they come for it in person — where you are standing, not somewhere you have to drive to. See them off and interest stops for a few days. Don't, and they take your best worker.
Wages, bail and fines you can't cover become debt. Debt compounds at 8% a day, at midnight, whether you're playing or not. At $150,000 the operation folds and the save is over.
It's slow enough to climb out of and fast enough to notice — which is the only thing that makes the risky choices feel like choices.
